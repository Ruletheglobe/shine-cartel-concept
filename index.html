Your bank information is encrypted using SSL technology, and The Shine Cartel never knows or stores it.

What do I do if I put in the wrong address?

Get in touch with info@theshinecartel.com right away. If your order hasn't already been sent out, we may be able to change the address for you.

My order never arrived. Shipment got lost or was stolen. What do I do?

Please email us directly at info@theshinecartel.com with your claim number and details about your situation.

How do I track my order?

For online orders, shipping confirmation will be sent in an email. Still have a question? Send us an email at info@theshinecartel.com.

Can I cancel my order?

We're sorry to hear that you'd like to cancel your order. Please email us at info@theshinecartel.com. We can only cancel orders that haven't been shipped yet, so hurry!

If a product goes out of stock during a promotion, can I apply the promo when it's back?

Sorry, we cannot offer gifts or discounts after the promotion has expired.
